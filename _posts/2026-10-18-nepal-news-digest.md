          <p class="story-summary">A preliminary National Planning Commission survey estimates the damage from the Bhotekoshi floods at Rs 79.17 billion across four districts, of which Rs 69.57 billion fell on households and Rs 9.60 billion on businesses and other establishments. Of 4,151 damaged structures, 2,989 (72 percent) were completely destroyed and about 77 percent are unusable.</p>

          <strong>Loss and Damage Fund calls extraordinary meeting for Oct 22 to support Nepal</strong>
          <p class="story-summary">The Fund for Responding to Loss and Damage will hold its first-ever extraordinary board meeting virtually on October 22 after Nepal's August 31 request for urgent help following the August 26 ice-and-rock avalanche. The board may provisionally set aside $21.6 million, including $20 million for Nepal, and pilot a way to accept Nepal's request outside the regular submission window. The government estimates economic loss and damage at a minimum of $2.69 billion and recovery needs at $4.71 billion, and advocates call $20 million a small fraction of that. A senior official said the decision will be made on October 22 but formally announced at COP31.</p>
