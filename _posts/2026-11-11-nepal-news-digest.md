            <p class="story-summary">Nepal marked Bhai Tika on Wednesday, the last day of Tihar, the country's second-largest festival, observed on Yama Dwitiya in the month of Kartik. Sisters apply seven- or five-coloured tika on their brothers' foreheads, praying for long life, health and prosperity, and brothers reciprocate. The Nepal Calendar Determination Development Committee set 11:07am as the most auspicious time, though the ritual can be performed at any hour of the day. The committee advised sisters to face east and brothers to face west while exchanging tika.</p>
