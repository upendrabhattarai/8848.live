          <strong>Narayani River Floods Hit Record High, Nears/Crosses Danger Level at Gandak Barrage</strong>
          <p class="story-summary">Flood conditions on the Narayani river system worsened rapidly through the day: myRepublica reported flow at the India-border Gandak Barrage reached 273,600 cusecs Saturday morning, just short of the 300,000-cusec warning threshold, while flow at Devghat on the Nepal side was already 348,000 cusecs and rising. The Narayani Basin Office warned that continued rainfall in the hills could push levels higher, and authorities relocated residents from Bharatpur wards 1, 2, 16 and 17 as homes there were inundated.
