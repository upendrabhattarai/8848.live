          <p class="story-summary">A preliminary Rapid Damage and Needs Assessment (RDNA) estimates the August 26 floods caused Rs408 billion in total damage and economic losses across five districts, with nearly Rs300 billion — more than two-thirds — falling on private businesses, households and privately owned infrastructure. Nearly 4,800 businesses were damaged (Rs146.19 billion in losses), with hotels and restaurants hit hardest (1,259 establishments), while 13 hydropower projects and five solar plants with a combined 759 MW capacity sustained Rs151 billion in damage, the single costliest sectoral loss. A total of 7,570 homes were damaged across Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Gorkha and Chitwan, and total reconstruction is projected to cost Rs723.31 billion; the Cabinet has already approved a first-phase recovery package of tax relief and concessional loans for affected businesses.</p>
