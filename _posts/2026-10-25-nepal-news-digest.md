          <strong>Loss and Damage Fund allocates $20 million to Nepal for Bhotekoshi flood recovery</strong>
          <p class="story-summary">The Fund for Responding to Loss and Damage approved $20 million (about Rs 3 billion) for relief, rehabilitation and reconstruction in the flood-hit Bhotekoshi-Trishuli region at a special board meeting on October 22. Nepal's agriculture and finance ministers had written to the fund on August 31 seeking urgent help after the August 26 floods, which directly affected more than 60,000 people from about 15,000 households. Setopati reports that the proposal passed without formal objection, but the money is not immediate: Nepal must submit a formal proposal with its spending areas and implementing bodies, and the board must approve it before release. The sum, plus a $1.6 million supervision fee, covers only about 0.42 percent of Nepal's estimated $4.78 billion reconstruction need.</p>

          <strong>Bay of Bengal cyclone could bring rain and snow to Nepal from October 28 to November 1</strong>
          <p class="story-summary">A low-pressure system over the Bay of Bengal may strengthen into a cyclonic storm and bring rain and snow to Nepal from October 28 to November 1, according to the Department of Hydrology and Meteorology. Meteorologist Binod Pokharel expects the highest rainfall risk on October 30 and 31, flagging Kathmandu and 28 districts across Koshi, Madhesh, Bagmati, Gandaki and Lumbini provinces, with Manang needing particular attention. Experts urged farmers to harvest and protect standing paddy and crops drying in the fields, and advised trekkers to be cautious, with one recommending that high-Himalayan treks be suspended during the period.</p>
