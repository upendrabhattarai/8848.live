          <p class="story-summary">At least 21 people died across western and south-central Nepal after rain that began Thursday, with more than 1,500 people from 747 households displaced. Six bridges were fully damaged and 44 livestock were lost. Online Khabar later reported that the Baglung toll had reached 12 with one person still missing, with Tarakhola rural municipality the hardest hit.</p>
