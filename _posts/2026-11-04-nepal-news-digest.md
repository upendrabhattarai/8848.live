          <strong>Government removes IGP Karki, names Manoj KC police chief</strong>
          <p class="story-summary">An emergency Cabinet meeting on Wednesday, November 4, removed Inspector General of Police Dan Bahadur Karki and appointed Additional IGP Manoj KC, long favoured by Prime Minister Balendra Shah's camp, to lead the force. Karki was moved to the post of security adviser to the prime minister, with about 17 months left in his tenure. The Post reports that officials pointed to his handling of the Inam Sapkota probe and his resistance to immediate arrest orders. Government spokesperson Sasmit Pokharel said only responsibilities were reshuffled and denied any dissatisfaction with Karki.</p>
